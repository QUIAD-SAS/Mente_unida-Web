## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an empathetic, grounded, and clinical-yet-warm digital sanctuary for mental healthcare. By rejecting the sterile, utilitarian sterility of traditional medical software, the visual atmosphere communicates psychological safety, human warmth, and professional credibility.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Modernism** with **Gentle Tactile Surfaces**:
- **Atmosphere:** Natural, restorative, deeply humane, and unhurried.
- **Target Audience:** Patients navigating vulnerability, anxiety, or transition seeking qualified therapists, paired with licensed psychologists and clinicians requiring a dignified, reliable clinical portal.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Calm assurance, grounded validation, clarity without sensory overload, and ethical rigor.

## Colors

The palette balances restorative botanical depth with sun-warmed organic accents:

- **Primary Teal (`#1D5C56`):** The cornerstone of safety, balance, and quiet confidence. Used for primary CTAs, active highlights, key navigation markers, and verified credibility touchpoints.
- **Secondary Terracotta (`#E07A5F`):** Infuses organic warmth, empathy, human approachability, and supportive interactive accents.
- **Deep Forest Slate / Professional Surface (`#153935`):** Reserved for high-authority contexts, dedicated professional zones, footer architectures, and high-impact clinician modules.
- **Background Tiers:**
  - `surface-canvas`: Soft porcelain/linen ivory (`#FAF8F5`) for full-canvas soothing neutrality.
  - `surface-card`: Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) to elevate readability and content hierarchy.
  - `surface-subtle`: Muted warm sand-linen (`#F5F1EB`) for nested containers and search filters.
- **Text & Borders:**
  - `text-primary`: Deep slate charcoal (`#1C2524`) delivering AAA contrast.
  - `text-secondary`: Muted botanical slate (`#5A6865`) for supporting copy and clinical metadata.
  - `border-soft`: Warm stone edge (`#E5DFD6`) for soft, low-contrast structural boundaries.

## Typography

The pairing between **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter** creates a balance between gentle human expression and precision.

- **Headlines & Expressive Titles:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Its geometric clarity and open humanist apertures communicate optimism, empathy, and clarity. Headings maintain controlled negative tracking to avoid loose, clinical coldness.
- **Body & Clinical Descriptions:** Set in Inter at a generous `1.6` line height (`26px` on `16px` body). This delivers fatigue-free readability, vital for anxious or stressed users reading introspective diagnostic forms or therapist profiles.
- **Pills, Badges & Interactive Controls:** Set in medium-weight Plus Jakarta Sans with subtle positive tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.02em`) to guarantee quick recognition on smaller screen footprints.

## Layout & Spacing

A calming interface requires generous white space to prevent cognitive overwhelm.

- **Grid Philosophy:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container width capped at `1240px` for standard layouts and `1400px` for clinician dashboards.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` (20px), `gutter-sm: 1rem` (16px).
  - **Tablet (`768px – 1024px`):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem` (32px), `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
  - **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12 columns, `margin: 3rem` (48px), `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
- **Rhythm & Safe Margins:** Sections demand high vertical breathability (`4rem` to `6rem` vertical padding) to preserve an unhurried, reassuring pace throughout user onboarding and therapist selection.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid intimidating clinical rigidity, depth is constructed via **Tonal Stratification** paired with **Warm Ambient Diffusions**. Avoid stark drop shadows and sharp dark borders.

- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** `background: #FAF8F5`. No shadow. Baseline canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** `background: #FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #E5DFD6`, shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.04)`. A gentle warm-teal-tinted ambient floor.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Modals):** `background: #FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #DFD8CE`, shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(224, 122, 95, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Toasts, Popups & Verification Flyouts):** `background: #FFFFFF`, shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(21, 57, 53, 0.12)`.
- **Inverted Dark Elevation (Clinician / Pro Surface):** Base `background: #153935`. Nested cards use a lighter tinted surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with subtle inner glow `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes reassurance through organic, soft geometries:

- **Base Radius:** `0.5rem` (8px) for compact elements such as input controls, select pickers, and small tags.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) to `1.25rem` (20px) across provider profile cards, session bookings, and dialogue modals, softening hard digital edges.
- **Badge & Pill Elements:** Fully rounded capsule/pill styles (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) reserved for verification markers, status chips, and step progression trackers.
- **Micro-interactivity:** Focus rings utilize a smooth, rounded outline offset of `2px` with a calming primary teal tint (`rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.35)`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled Warm Deep Teal (`#1D5C56`), text white, `rounded-lg` (12px) or pill (24px) for landing conversions. On hover: subtle lift (`translateY(-1px)`) with shadow deepening (`rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary (Action / Booking):** Warm Terracotta (`#E07A5F`), white text. Used for immediate human actions (e.g., "Agendar Sesión", "Hablar con un terapeuta").
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #E5DFD6`, text `#1D5C56`. Hover fills with `rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.04)` and border darkens to `#1D5C56`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#1D5C56`. Hover: `rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.06)` background.

### Chips & Verification Badges
- **"Profesional Verificado" Seal:** Pill shape (`9999px`), `background: rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.08)`, border: `1px solid rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.2)`, text: `#1D5C56`. Accompanied by a solid checkmark icon inside an emerald-teal disc.
- **Process Steps ("01. Escuchar"):** Pill badge, subtle terracotta tint `background: rgba(224, 122, 95, 0.1)`, text `#C65C40`, font weight 600.
- **Filter Tags:** Neutral stone pill (`#F5F1EB`), text `#5A6865`. When active: solid `#1D5C56`, text white.

### Form Inputs
- **Base State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5DFD6`, text `#1C2524`, padding `0.875rem 1rem`, rounded `10px`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#1D5C56`, outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 92, 86, 0.12)`. No abrupt high-contrast browser outlines.
- **Labels & Hints:** Labels sit above inputs in Plus Jakarta Sans 14px semi-bold (`#1C2524`); helper notes in `#5A6865`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom rounded squares (checkbox, `6px` radius) and circles (radio).
- Inactive: Border `1.5px solid #C4BCB1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Active: Background `#1D5C56`, border `#1D5C56`, white indicator checkmark or dot.

### Provider Profile Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `18px`, border `1px solid #E5DFD6`, padding `1.5rem`.
- Features circular therapist avatar with soft border, "Profesional Verificado" pill badge, primary therapeutic specialty tag, rating display, and direct CTA to schedule consultation.

### Modals & Dialogs (Authentication & Onboarding)
- **Overlay:** Muted deep charcoal backdrop with gentle blur (`rgba(28, 37, 36, 0.45)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`).
- **Surface:** Rounded `24px`, background `#FAF8F5`, inner content card `#FFFFFF`, with reassuring copy welcoming the user into a safe, confidential environment.
